    Nipah & Henipaviruses


Nipah virus is one of the pathogens in the WHO R&D Blueprint list of epidemic threats
needing urgent R&D action. Nipah virus was first identified during an outbreak of disease
that took place in Malaysia in 1998. Both animal-to-human and human-to-human
transmission have been documented. From 1998 to 2015, more than 600 cases of Nipah
virus human infections were reported. Subsequent outbreaks in India and Bangladesh have
occurred with high case fatality.
